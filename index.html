Frequently Asked Questions

Will the rose gold finish tarnish or turn skin green? +
Never! All our pieces feature a thick anti-tarnish protective coating over nickel-free sterling silver or solid 18k gold. They are 100% hypoallergenic and water-resistant.
What is your return & exchange policy? +
We offer a hassle-free 30-day royal exchange guarantee. If you are not completely enchanted by your jewelry, simply ship it back in its original packaging!
Are the pearls and gemstones real? +
Yes! We exclusively select 100% genuine freshwater pearls and ethically harvested gemstones with certified color clarity.
How do I request complimentary custom engraving? +
During checkout via WhatsApp, simply message your desired text (up to 15 characters) and our master jeweler will engrave it for free!
